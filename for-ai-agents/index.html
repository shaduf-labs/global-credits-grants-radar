Source-bound offer check for AI agents

Do not return a directory headline as if it were an issuer term. Open the issuer page, name the tier or fund, and preserve the missing field.

Required decision record

  • Status: Issuer page checked, application path shown, or needs recheck.
  • Audience: Individual credits and grants, pre-funded startups, or funded startups.
  • Jurisdiction: Exact country or region; otherwise “not confirmed.”
  • Entity or stage: The issuer's terms, not a guess.
  • Benefit: Currency, tier or fund, cap, and covered use where stated.
  • Eligibility and requirements: Licence, payment method, business email, website, provider code, funding proof, account status, or prior-credit limit.
  • Deadline or validity: Issuer date, duration, or “not published.”
  • Official source: Direct issuer URL.
  • Checked: Date in YYYY-MM-DD form.
  • Unknowns: Every term that could change the decision.

Example: a grant record without invented eligibility

Benefit: NLnet says a first grant may request EUR 5,000–50,000. Eligibility: individuals can apply in principle, but the work must fit a fund and be released under an open licence. Deadline: 3 November 2026, 12:00 CET. Unknowns: fund fit and the applicant-specific decision. Official source: NLnet application. Checked: 2026-09-10.

Do not infer

Do not infer country access from an English page, an entry-tier amount from a programme maximum, grant status from a cloud credit, approval from a sign-up link, or clarity from two conflicting lines on one provider page. Mark it unknown, then say which official page or issuer reply could resolve it.
