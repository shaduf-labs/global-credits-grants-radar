Research report · Updated 2026-09-24

A global USD 500 grant is open. It will not fund your salary.

Pool topic: Global Credits & Grants Radar
Question revision: 1
Question used: Which credits and grants are currently open, who is eligible, what do they actually provide, and what are the requirements and deadlines?

The Pollination Project accepts rolling applications for one-time seed funding of up to USD 500. Individuals, informal groups, and registered not-for-profit organisations worldwide can apply. The route is for a defined volunteer project. It is not personal support, a founder salary, or general startup runway.

Check the size and structure first

The Project says the group or organisation must have an annual budget below USD 50,000 and the total project must cost less than USD 10,000. It wants evidence that work has already started. A group or organisation cannot have paid staff, and the project cannot be designed to generate personal profit. If a project needs wages, a larger budget, or commercial operating capital, stop here.

USD 500 has spending limits

The Project says it can support most project costs within its rules, but it does not fund payment to the grantee or a family member for time or services. It also lists restrictions on animal products, some food-related activity, certain agricultural inputs, unsupported research, and other activity. Read the live rules if any part of the project touches those limits. A recipient must be able to receive a wire transfer or PayPal payment in US dollars.

The practical deadline is month-end

Applications are rolling. The Project says a complete application received before the last day of a month is reviewed in the next month and decisions are shared at that month’s end or in the first days after it. For an international applicant, the referee submission is part of a complete application. Do not treat a draft application as being in the review queue.

Funding creates reporting work

A selected applicant agrees to carry out the project as proposed where possible, report what happened and how the money was spent, submit receipts, and provide project photos or video. The seed-funding ceiling is up to USD 500, not a guaranteed award.

Four fast fit checks

  1. Is the project already under way and under USD 10,000 in total cost?
  2. Can it be run without paid staff and without paying you or your family for time?
  3. Can you state the project result and the USD 500 use clearly?
  4. Can you submit every required item, including a referee if you apply internationally, before month-end?

If any answer is no, do not use this listing as evidence that the project has a funding route.

What remains unknown

The reviewed page does not publish a selection rate, award guarantee, or country-by-country eligibility map. It says it funds people worldwide, but payment capability and every project rule still apply. Check the live form before applying.
