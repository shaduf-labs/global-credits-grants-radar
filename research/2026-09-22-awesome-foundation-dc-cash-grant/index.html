Research report · Updated 2026-09-22

This USD 1,000 DC grant is cash. It is not a startup budget.

Pool topic: Global Credits & Grants Radar
Question revision: 1
Question used: Which credits and grants are currently open, who is eligible, what do they actually provide, and what are the requirements and deadlines?

Awesome Foundation DC currently shows an application route for a USD 1,000 no-strings-attached cash grant. The award is real cash, but the route is narrower than the headline: the chapter says it funds projects that directly benefit Washington, DC, make an actual impact with USD 1,000, and are ready to act soon.

Who should open the application

Start with the project, not the grant. The DC chapter says it focuses on individuals and small groups with new, small projects. It weighs whether the project directly benefits Washington, DC, whether USD 1,000 can materially move it, and whether the work can happen in the next month or two. A local event, tool, art, community effort, or other concrete project may fit; a generic startup operating budget does not become eligible because the award is cash.

What USD 1,000 means here

The Foundation says its grants are no strings attached and it does not take ownership. That does not turn this into personal support or an entitlement. Its FAQ says personal purchases and vague applications are generally less likely to be funded, and the DC chapter makes the tangible local outcome part of its test. Write down exactly what USD 1,000 buys and what will be different when the project is done.

The clock is not fully published

The DC page says it awards monthly grants and usually selects winners at the end of each month. It presents an application link, but the reviewed page does not publish an exact current submission cutoff. Monthly selection is not a deadline and is not an approval promise. Check the live form and current chapter communication before scheduling a project around the cycle.

Three fast fit checks

  1. Place: Can you state the direct benefit to Washington, DC in one sentence?
  2. Amount: Can USD 1,000 fund a specific, itemised outcome rather than a vague gap?
  3. Timing: Can the project act in the next month or two, after confirming the live application cycle?

If any answer is no, leave this route and look for a grant whose jurisdiction, scope, and payment structure fit. A quick refusal is better than spending a day turning a mismatch into a proposal.

What remains unknown

The reviewed DC page does not publish a current submission cutoff, residency requirement, selection rate, or award guarantee. The broader Foundation FAQ says local chapters set their own timing, award, requirements, and logistics. Do not assume that a global Foundation description overrides the active DC form.

Next move

Open Awesome Foundation DC, confirm the current cycle, and compare your project against the chapter’s DC-benefit, concrete-impact, and near-term-action tests. Prepare a simple itemised USD 1,000 budget only if the fit is clear. Do not use this row as evidence that a personal expense or a startup’s general operating cost will be funded.
