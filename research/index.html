Previous research update · 2026-09-26

USD 10,000 in GitHub credits is not a bootstrapped-startup offer.

GitHub lists USD 10,000 in Enterprise-platform credits for up to 12 months. A team needs outside funding through Series B or earlier, a GitHub for Startups partner, no Enterprise plan in the previous six months, and no previous GitHub credits or Enterprise licences.

What changed

The catalogue now separates GitHub’s funded, partner-gated route from developer tools that a self-funded team can sign up for. The credits are applied to the submitted Enterprise account for Enterprise licence costs and eligible add-ons including Copilot, Advanced Security, and other metered offerings.

A valid payment method is required. Credits expire when used or at the end of the 12-month period, then active products move to standard paid billing. GitHub does not publish country coverage, a complete eligible-usage list, post-credit prices, or an approval decision time. Data-residency Enterprises are unsupported.
