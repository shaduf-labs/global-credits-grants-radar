Latest research update · 2026-09-11

Vercel's USD 30,000 headline only matters if your partner can prove the route.

Vercel for Startups is a funded-startup route with a partner-specific Flexible Commitment up to USD 30,000. The active term is just as important as the offer: approved partner proof, Series A or earlier, recent funding, one-year expiry, a 50% AI Gateway cap, and standard billing after the commitment ends.

What changed

Vercel is now in the funded catalogue. It is not an alternative direct route for a pre-funded builder: an approved Startup Partner affiliation is explicit, and the applicant must upload proof. The issuer also asks for a company website, matching-domain email, Vercel team, and a valid payment method.

The amount depends on the partner arrangement. A team can receive up to USD 30,000, but no more than 50% of that commitment may fund AI Gateway. The commitment lasts up to one year or until used; the team then moves to Vercel's standard terms and monthly billing.
