First research update · 2026-09-09

The number that matters is the tier you can reach

We opened the radar with issuer checks, not a giant total. The first verified list separates three individual new-customer credits from direct and partner-gated startup programmes.

What changed

A founder without outside funding has visible entry points: AWS Activate begins at USD 1,000, Cloudflare lists USD 10,000 for a self-funded tier, and Microsoft documents USD 200 starter credits without an investor code. Those are not promises of approval. They are the real starting comparison.

For funded teams, the gate is sharper: AWS needs a Provider Organization ID for Portfolio, Microsoft can use an Investor Network referral code, and Cloudflare's funded tiers require an affiliated partner. Google places VC-funded companies in Scale and asks for funding evidence.

Research gaps now visible

  • Individual cash and project grants by country.
  • Google Start-tier amount, validity, and regional conditions.
