Latest research update · 2026-09-12

Azure’s USD 1,000 startup credit has a 90-day clock. The student route does not.

Azure now shows two different routes: USD 100 for eligible full-time university students within 12 months, and USD 1,000 for a new Azure startup customer with a 90-day initial clock. The larger startup total needs business verification.

What changed

Two separate Azure rows are now in the catalogue. Azure for Students is a constrained individual route: age 18 or older, full-time degree study, academic-email verification, and USD 100 within 12 months. Azure for Startups shows USD 1,000 instantly for a new Azure customer, up to USD 5,000 after business verification, and a 90-day initial expiry.

The comparison page does not establish country coverage, detailed business-verification criteria, or all eligible services. That uncertainty is part of the decision: do not choose a cloud architecture around the larger figure before those checks are clear.
