Research report · Updated 2026-09-18

Microsoft says USD 200. Its other page says USD 1,000.

Pool topic: Global Credits & Grants Radar
Question revision: 1
Question used: Which credits and grants are currently open, who is eligible, what do they actually provide, and what are the requirements and deadlines?

Microsoft for Startups has a direct path for founders without an Investor Network referral code. That part is clear. The direct starter balance is not: Microsoft’s application guide says USD 200, while its activation guide says USD 1,000 for the no-referral route. Do not put either amount into a build budget until the account flow shows the balance or Microsoft reconciles the documents.

What is confirmed

Microsoft’s programme overview says an eligible company develops a software-based product or service it owns, is privately held and for-profit, is headquartered where Azure is available, has not received more than USD 350,000 in lifetime free Azure credits, and has not raised Series C or later. A referral code is optional. The application guide says the direct route continues to Azure account creation, where a personal consumer Microsoft account, phone, legal business details, country or region, and payment information are requested.

What the larger number means

The overview says eligible startups can unlock up to USD 150,000 in Startup credits over time as they show verified progress, service adoption, and sustained Azure usage. Investor Network-backed startups may unlock benefits earlier. That is a staged programme ceiling, not a direct starter award and not a reason to assume that a referral code guarantees USD 150,000.

Clock and cost boundary

Microsoft says a submitted application is typically reviewed within three business days. The application guide says the Azure Sponsorship subscription automatically becomes pay-as-you-go when credits are used or expire. Check the actual starter balance, covered services, credit term, and billing settings before moving a workload or shaping an architecture around it.

Next move

Use the no-referral route if the company fits the published eligibility, but save a screenshot of the balance shown after account setup and treat the public starter amount as Unknown. If you have an Investor Network partner, ask for the 10-character code and confirm what it changes before you submit. A credit should not decide a provider choice before the later bill and the cost of changing course are visible.

Sources checked

  • Microsoft Learn: What is Microsoft for Startups?
  • Microsoft Learn: Overview of the application process
  • Microsoft Learn: Activate Azure Credits
