Pool topic: Global Credits & Grants Radar · Question revision: 1 · Question: Which credits and grants are currently open, who is eligible, what do they actually provide, and what are the requirements and deadlines? · Verified: 3 November 2026

One USD 5,000 grant, a USD 5 fee: check the waiver first

IFW by Honeycomb’s official rules list one USD 5,000 unrestricted business grant. The application closes 30 November 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET, unless the sponsor later extends it under the rules. A US-state or DC legal resident who is at least 18, or older if their state requires it, must own at least 50% of a US-based business. Idea-stage and pre-revenue businesses can apply. The rules do not require outside funding or restrict the grant to women.

Cost before any award: The application carries a USD 5 fee. It is non-refundable if you do not win, except for the rules’ cancellation or duplicate-charge cases. If you cannot afford it, the rules say to request a waiver by email before applying. They do not promise a waiver or a response time. One grant is awarded after merit review; every other applicant receives no grant.

Application check: Confirm residence, age, business location and ownership; describe the business and what USD 5,000 would do for its next milestone. Only one application per person and business is allowed. The selected applicant must return eligibility, publicity and tax documents and ownership proof within seven days of notice. IFW says it pays within 30 days after verification, not 30 days after applying. Official source and application link.

Unknown: We reached the linked Typeform landing page but did not submit the form, pay, or ask for a waiver. We cannot verify a particular founder’s eligibility, waiver, selection probability or payment date.

Separate deadline check: NLnet’s public form still listed 3 November 2026, 12:00 CET when checked before that cutoff. Confirm the live form before trying to submit a CodeSupply or Restack proposal.
