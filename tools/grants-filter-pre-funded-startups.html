Kiro is not a second direct credit

Kiro’s startup page says its separate Pro+ promotion is for VC-backed teams and excludes active AWS Activate credit holders. Do not count it as a self-funded award or add it to an existing Activate balance. AWS says Activate credits can cover Kiro. Check the company-domain, AWS account and paid-billing terms before choosing a route.

Bruin’s credit has a backing gate

Bruin lists USD 10,000 for new users backed by YC, Techstars, or Antler, not a direct self-funded award. If you are not eligible, its official page offers a waitlist, an open-source CLI, and a free Cloud tier. Do not count a waitlist place as credit runway.

A grant for a local fintech project, not a cloud budget

Interledger names local fintech startups, but the project must serve open payments, digital financial access, open-source innovation, financial inclusion, or digital public infrastructure. Applications are paused as of 29 September, despite the earlier 11 November date. Do not prepare the concept note until the issuer announces reopening.

A direct application can still have an unknown award

DigitalOcean lets an unbacked founder submit, but it does not publish a fixed award. MongoDB has the same decision shape: a direct route with a benefit whose base amount is not public. Apply only if the product works without the hoped-for balance.

Atlas credits do not pay Atlas fees

Atlas charges USD 500 to incorporate and USD 100 annually for registered-agent service after the first year. Its USD 2,500 offer offsets eligible Stripe product fees after incorporation, not those Atlas charges. Check whether the Delaware entity and Stripe account fit your needs before paying. Read the issuer-term check.

Coverage and billing are separate questions

For Cloudflare, ask how the recent-funding rule applies, which products are covered at your tier, what the cap is, and when a payment method is charged. For DigitalOcean, standard credits exclude GPU products and third-party AI models outside DigitalOcean infrastructure. A credit headline is not a service contract. Microsoft’s USD 200 and USD 1,000 direct-starter conflict is the same kind of warning: the account display, coverage, and billing setting decide what can safely be planned.

Application packet

Keep one folder with registration details, public site, product summary, funding statement, cloud-account identifiers, previous credits, and expected spend inside each credit window. For AWS Activate, verify that the site is publicly functional before you submit.

Already using Microsoft startup credits?

The Microsoft FAQ says credits cannot be extended or transferred. Check the balance, expiry, covered services and billing profile in your account. The USD 1,000 plus conditional USD 4,000 new-to-Azure offer is not a renewal for an existing recipient; the Investor Network offer needs a referral code. The issuer’s no-code guides still conflict on USD 200 versus USD 1,000, so confirm the actual balance before spending. Verified 2026-10-05.
