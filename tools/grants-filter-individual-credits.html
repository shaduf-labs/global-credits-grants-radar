Global seed-grant preflight

First test the boundary: is the project already under way, under USD 10,000 in total cost, volunteer-led, and non-profit? Can you explain what USD 500 pays for without charging for your own time? If yes, confirm you can receive a US-dollar wire or PayPal payment and submit every required item by month-end. International applicants need a referee submission before the application is complete.

DC microgrant preflight

First test the fit: does the project directly benefit Washington, DC, does USD 1,000 buy a specific outcome, and can the work happen in the next month or two? If yes, open the live form and check the current cycle before writing. Keep a simple itemised budget and do not use the broad “no strings attached” label to imply an award is automatic, a personal-expense payment, or startup operating capital.

Student-credit preflight

Prove current enrollment before you count either Pack credit. Then check one live partner claim at a time. For Stripe, confirm the account and country fit, covered fee, expiry, and fee after credit before you connect a payment flow. For Heroku, check the plan, country, rollover, payment method, and post-credit price. A payment-fee credit and a monthly hosting credit are separate, bounded experiments; neither is a lump-sum budget.

Eligible is not the same as a fit

Oracle has a published trial, but country availability, card verification, the permanent Home Region, and capacity decide if it is a sensible test. OTF permits individuals, but it funds a defined internet-freedom effort and pays against a project contract. UKRI welcomes individuals, yet a UK research organisation must host the project lead. NLnet can fund an individual, but only an open-technology project that fits a fund. A personal eligibility label never replaces a scope, account, or host check.

Oracle preflight

Before creating the account, confirm that your country is offered, choose the Home Region for the workload you actually plan to test, identify the service that consumes the trial, check its capacity, and decide what you will export or shut down when the credit ends. Oracle says it will not charge the verification card unless you upgrade, but that does not make post-trial resources free.

Unknowns

We have not mapped every active country-specific grant, individual eligibility case, Pollination Project country-by-country eligibility rule, Awesome Foundation chapter cycle, GitHub-Pack partner term, payment method, product exclusion, regional capacity condition, or individual project fund. The Stripe row does not prove account access or a free payment flow in your country; the Heroku row does not prove coverage in your country or a free post-credit deployment. OTF is not a worldwide inventory of general personal grants; country-specific research remains a priority.
