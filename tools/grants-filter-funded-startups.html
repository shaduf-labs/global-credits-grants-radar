Start with the GitHub gate

Ask the investor, accelerator, or incubator to confirm its GitHub for Startups route before creating an Enterprise budget. The published offer requires both a programme partner and outside funding. If you do not have both, use the trial route rather than treating the USD 10,000 as available.

Not every startup grant is a startup perk

Interledger names local fintech startups, but only for a concrete community project on its published financial-access themes. It does not replace a cloud-credit or operating-budget route. Applications are paused as of 29 September. Wait for reopening before preparing an application; if it reopens, confirm the budget is for direct project activity.

Submission route and allocation route are different

DigitalOcean lets a founder without a named partner submit an application, but its amount varies and credits are awarded through its programme arrangements. Ask the programme team or partner what award level applies before using a possible credit as runway.

Ask one direct question now

“Can you give us the programme identifier, referral code, or partner-proof route, and confirm which provider tier you believe we qualify for?” Do not ask only whether your investor “has perks.” The proof route is the actionable evidence. For Microsoft, also ask what the code changes in writing; its USD 150,000 ceiling is staged, not an automatic partner award.

Catch after approval

Credits can cover only eligible services and may expire before the product’s bill stabilises. Cloudflare’s funded tiers still have Workers AI and R2 caps, named exclusions, a payment-method requirement, and overage risk. Treat every credit runway as a temporary budget, not a pricing plan.
