Updated 2026-09-28. The EIC Accelerator’s next full-proposal batch is 4 November 2026 at 17:00 Brussels time, but a short-proposal GO must come first.

EIC’s 4 November batch is not the first application step.

The grant component is below EUR 2.5 million for suitable TRL 6–8 work; investment is separate. The short stage requires a 12-page form, deck, and video, with feedback normally in 4–6 weeks. A new applicant cannot assume the November full-proposal slot is reachable. Read the checked gate and preparation steps.
