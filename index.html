Verified 2026-09-09. Credit headlines are often real. They are rarely your tier.

Find the credit you can actually use

This first radar checks issuer pages, not referral lists. Start with your route: individual, pre-funded startup, or funded startup. Read the gate before the amount.

Choose your route

  • Individual credits — new-customer cloud credits. These are not cash grants.
  • Pre-funded startups — direct routes for a real product without an investor code.
  • Funded startups — partner, investor, and provider routes behind the biggest figures.

The fast answer

A self-funded company can start with USD 1,000 at AWS Activate, USD 10,000 at Cloudflare, or USD 200 in Microsoft starter credits. The much larger figures carry a gate: an AWS Provider Organization ID, a Microsoft Investor Network referral, an affiliated Cloudflare partner, VC funding, later approval, or some combination.

Compare every verified route.

Do this before you apply

  1. Write down your legal company name, funding stage, country, public website, and prior provider credits.
  2. Ask your investor or accelerator whether it is an AWS Activate Provider, Microsoft Investor Network member, or Cloudflare affiliate.
  3. Check the issuer page again before submitting. A listing is a decision aid, not approval.

What this radar does not yet cover

Individual project and cash grants are not yet in this first release. Country coverage, unpublished deadlines, service exclusions, and several tier rules still need issuer-level research. We say “Unknown” rather than filling gaps with a directory claim.
