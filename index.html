Updated 2026-09-12. Azure’s USD 1,000 startup credit is a 90-day decision, not a vague “free cloud” promise.

Find the funding route you can actually use

Microsoft now shows two separate Azure routes: USD 100 for eligible full-time university students over 12 months, and USD 1,000 instantly for a new Azure startup customer, with up to USD 5,000 after business verification. Before you use either, check the recipient rule, expiry clock, covered use, and bill after credit.

Choose your route

  • Individual credits & grants — student and new-customer credits plus an open-technology project grant.
  • Pre-funded startups — direct routes with the expiry clock beside the headline amount.
  • Funded startups — partner, investor, and provider routes behind the biggest figures.

The fast answer

For individuals, Azure for Students is USD 100 within 12 months only for eligible full-time university students. For pre-funded startups, Azure for Startups shows USD 1,000 immediately for a new Azure customer and up to USD 5,000 after business verification, but the initial credit lasts 90 days. For funded startups, Vercel's partner-gated route is up to USD 30,000 for up to one year, but the amount varies by partner and AI Gateway can use no more than 50% of the commitment. Cloudflare still needs a direct check for a fully self-funded applicant because its page pairs a self-funded Tier 3 with a general recent-funding requirement.

Compare every verified route.

Do this before you apply

  1. For a partner offer, ask the investor, accelerator, or partner team for its programme route and the proof the issuer will accept.
  2. Keep your public website, business email, funding statement, cloud-account identifier, and prior-credit history ready.
  3. Write down the credit end date, covered services, spend caps, and the cost after it ends before you add usage.

What this radar does not yet cover

One individual project grant is not a global grants inventory. Country coverage, other grant types, service exclusions, and several tier rules still need issuer-level research. We say “Unknown” rather than filling gaps with a directory claim.
